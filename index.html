The Artist Behind the Brush

Meet Mabel

With a passion for beauty that runs deeper than pigment and powder, Mabel Aphane has built a reputation for transformative artistry that honours the individuality of every client. Based in Johannesburg, Mabel brings precision, warmth, and creative vision to every chair.

Her philosophy is simple but profound: makeup is not about covering up — it is about revealing your most confident self. Whether you're walking down the aisle, stepping in front of a camera, or attending the event of the season, Mabel ensures every detail is intentional, flattering, and uniquely yours.

Trained in industry-leading techniques and continuously inspired by global beauty trends, Mabel blends contemporary artistry with a deep understanding of diverse skin tones — delivering results that are always camera-ready and celebration-worthy.
